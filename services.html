Advisory Services

A Complete Suite for Complex Asset Transactions

From initial valuation through final disposition, 1 Oak Advisory provides banks and credit unions with the full spectrum of advisory capabilities required to execute complex asset transactions with precision and confidence.

Asset Valuation

Rigorous, market-informed valuation of loan portfolios, individual notes, REO holdings, and distressed instruments. Our valuations are data-driven, defensible, and calibrated to current market conditions — designed to support institutional decision-making and regulatory scrutiny.

Portfolio Analysis

Comprehensive review and stratification of asset portfolios to identify risk profiles, recovery potential, and optimal disposition pathways. We deliver clear, actionable intelligence — organized to support board-level reporting and executive decision-making.

Asset Sale Facilitation

End-to-end transaction management from initial asset positioning through final closing. We coordinate all parties, manage the diligence process, and drive execution to maximize proceeds while minimizing timeline and institutional burden.

Note Sales

Full-service execution on performing, sub-performing, and non-performing note transactions. We package, position, and market note pools with access to a deep network of qualified institutional buyers — achieving competitive pricing through structured, confidential processes.

REO Disposition

Strategic disposition of real estate owned assets through our established network of institutional and private capital buyers. We balance speed of execution with price maximization, providing a structured process that protects the institution's position from listing through close.

Distressed Asset Advisory

Advisory counsel at every stage of a distressed situation — from early workout strategy through final resolution. We help institutions evaluate alternatives, model recovery outcomes, and execute with confidence, whether through sale, modification, or litigation support.

BPO Coordination

Coordination and management of Broker Price Opinion engagements to support accurate asset valuation, regulatory compliance, and informed pricing decisions. We source, vet, and manage BPO providers to ensure quality and consistency across your portfolio.

Investor Network Access

Direct access to our curated network of qualified buyers across asset classes — community banks, credit unions, private equity firms, family offices, and institutional investors actively seeking financial assets. Every buyer in our network is pre-qualified and bound by confidentiality.
