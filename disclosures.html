Disclosures & Compliance

1 Oak Advisory buys residential mortgage assets as a principal, for its own account.

Important information about the nature of 1 Oak Advisory's business and the terms on which this website and its materials are provided. Please read this page carefully before submitting information to us or relying on anything presented here.

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Nature of Business

1 Oak Advisory LLC ("1 Oak," "we," or "the Company") acquires residential mortgage assets — principally residential second mortgages and home equity lines of credit — as a principal, for its own account, and manages those assets following purchase.

When we evaluate or purchase an asset, we do so as a buyer acting in our own interest. We are not acting as a broker, agent, fiduciary, or advisor for a seller, and we are not engaged to represent a seller's interests in a transaction. A seller and 1 Oak are counterparties to a proposed purchase and sale, and each party is responsible for its own analysis, pricing decisions, and negotiating position.

Sellers should evaluate any proposed transaction independently and with their own legal, accounting, tax, and financial advisors before agreeing to it.

Institutional Counterparties Only

This website and the materials made available through it are directed to mortgage originators, banks, credit unions, funds, and other institutional holders of residential mortgage assets. They are intended for use by those institutions and their professional advisors.

1 Oak does not originate consumer mortgage loans. Nothing on this website is an offer of credit, an offer to make or arrange a loan, a loan solicitation, or a communication directed to consumers or individual borrowers. Consumers and borrowers with questions about an existing loan should contact the servicer of that loan.

No Offer, Bid or Commitment

Nothing on this website is a bid, an offer, or a commitment to purchase any loan, pool, or other asset. No content here should be read as a promise that 1 Oak will buy anything, or buy it at any particular price or on any particular timetable.

Any indicative or preliminary pricing we provide is non-binding. It is based on information supplied by the seller, is prepared for discussion purposes only, and is subject to completion of due diligence, review of collateral and loan files, internal approval, and the negotiation and execution of definitive transaction documents. Indicative pricing may be revised or withdrawn at any time, including as a result of what diligence shows.

Information Provided by Sellers

In evaluating a proposed transaction, 1 Oak relies on the accuracy and completeness of the loan-level data, tapes, documents, and files provided by the seller or by parties acting on the seller's behalf.

We make no representation or warranty regarding information we have not independently verified, and preliminary pricing based on unverified information should not be treated as a valuation or as confirmation that the information is correct. Representations and warranties about the assets, and the remedies for a breach of them, are matters for the definitive transaction documents between the parties.

Confidentiality and Non-Public Personal Information

Loan tapes and loan files can contain borrower non-public personal information ("NPI"), including names, addresses, account numbers, credit information, and other sensitive data.

Please do not transmit borrower NPI — such as borrower names, Social Security numbers, or account numbers — through the form on this website or by unsecured email. Loan characteristics are sufficient for an initial review. Before files containing borrower detail are exchanged, we can arrange a non-disclosure agreement and a secure method of delivery.

Information a seller provides in connection with a proposed transaction is reviewed confidentially and used to evaluate that transaction. Each party remains responsible for its own obligations under applicable privacy, data-protection, and information-security laws, and for satisfying itself that any transfer of borrower information is permitted under those laws and under the seller's own agreements and policies.

No Legal, Tax, Accounting or Investment Advice

The content of this website is provided for general informational purposes only. It is not legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, or investment advice, and it does not take account of the circumstances of any particular institution. You should consult your own advisors before acting on anything you read here.

Nothing on this website is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security or other investment interest, and no part of this site should be construed as an offering of any fund, participation, or investment product.

No Relationship Without a Written Agreement

Visiting this website, submitting the contact form, sending a loan tape, or engaging in preliminary discussions with us does not create any contract, purchase obligation, exclusivity, right of first refusal, brokerage arrangement, or advisory or fiduciary relationship between you and 1 Oak.

Obligations between 1 Oak and a counterparty arise only under definitive written agreements — such as a non-disclosure agreement or a loan sale agreement — signed by authorized representatives of both parties, and only to the extent those agreements provide.

Licensing and Regulatory Status

1 Oak makes no representation on this website as to any registration, license, approval, or membership held or not held by the Company. Licensing and regulatory requirements for the purchase, ownership, and servicing of residential mortgage assets vary by state, by asset type, and by the role a party plays in a transaction, and those requirements change over time.

Counterparties should make their own determination as to the requirements applicable to a proposed transaction and should consult their own counsel. Questions about the Company's status in connection with a specific transaction may be directed to us using the contact details below and will be addressed directly rather than through this website.

They are based on information available at the time, are subject to change without notice, and are not guarantees or warranties of any result. Actual outcomes may differ materially, including as a result of diligence findings, market conditions, borrower performance, servicing or collateral issues, and legal or regulatory developments.
