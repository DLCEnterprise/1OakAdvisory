Disclosures & Compliance

Important information regarding the nature of 1 Oak Advisory's services, engagement terms, and applicable limitations. Please read carefully before engaging with any information presented on this website.

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

Nature of Services

1 Oak Advisory ("the Firm") provides specialized advisory services to banks, credit unions, and other federally or state-chartered financial institutions in connection with the valuation, analysis, and facilitation of asset transactions. The Firm's services include, but are not limited to, asset valuation, portfolio analysis, asset sale facilitation, note sales, REO disposition, distressed asset advisory, BPO coordination, and investor network access.

1 Oak Advisory acts as an advisory intermediary on behalf of the financial institution client. The Firm does not take title to any assets, does not hold client funds, and does not act as a principal in any transaction unless expressly agreed to in a separate written engagement agreement.

No Investment Advice

Nothing contained on this website, in any communication from 1 Oak Advisory, or in any materials provided by the Firm constitutes investment advice, legal advice, tax advice, or accounting advice. The information provided is for general informational purposes only and is not a solicitation or offer to buy or sell any security, financial instrument, or asset.

Recipients of information from 1 Oak Advisory are advised to consult with their own legal counsel, accountants, financial advisors, and other professional advisors before making any decisions based on such information. 1 Oak Advisory makes no representation or warranty as to the suitability of any transaction or asset disposition strategy for any particular institution.

Confidentiality Requirements

Confidentiality agreements are required prior to the review of any asset data, portfolio details, transaction information, or other non-public information provided by or on behalf of any financial institution.

All prospective clients, buyers, and transaction counterparties are required to execute a binding non-disclosure and confidentiality agreement before receiving any confidential information in connection with an engagement. This requirement is non-negotiable and applies to all parties without exception.

The unauthorized disclosure, reproduction, or use of any confidential information provided by 1 Oak Advisory or its clients is strictly prohibited and may subject the disclosing party to legal liability.

No Client Relationship Without Written Agreement

No advisory, fiduciary, or other professional relationship is created between 1 Oak Advisory and any person or institution by virtue of accessing this website, submitting an inquiry, or engaging in preliminary discussions with the Firm. A formal advisory relationship is established only upon the execution of a written engagement agreement signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

The Firm's total liability for any claim arising under an engagement agreement shall not exceed the fees actually paid by the client to the Firm in connection with the specific engagement giving rise to the claim.

1 Oak Advisory does not represent that it is registered as an investment adviser with the Securities and Exchange Commission, any state securities authority, or any other regulatory body.
