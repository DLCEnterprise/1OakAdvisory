About 1 Oak Advisory

Experience Across the Residential Mortgage Asset Lifecycle

Our primary focus is residential second mortgages and home equity lines of credit.

Founded in 2012, 1 Oak Advisory is a residential mortgage investment and asset management firm specializing in the acquisition and management of residential mortgage assets.

Our Background

Across the Full Asset Lifecycle

  • Acquisition
  • Underwriting
  • Servicing Oversight
  • Borrower Workouts
  • Modifications
  • Bankruptcy
  • Foreclosure
  • Asset Disposition

Over the years, our work has required us to operate across virtually every stage of the mortgage asset lifecycle — from acquisition and underwriting through servicing oversight, borrower workouts, modifications, bankruptcy, foreclosure, and eventual asset disposition.

That experience shapes how we invest today.

Our Philosophy

Behind Every Loan

Mortgage assets cannot always be understood by looking at a spreadsheet. Behind every loan is:

  • A borrower.
  • A property.
  • A lien structure.
  • A servicing history.
  • A collateral file.
  • And ultimately, a resolution strategy.

Understanding how those pieces interact is critical to properly evaluating residential mortgage assets.
That is where experience matters.

— 1 Oak Advisory

Second Liens

Specialized Experience in Second Liens

  • The first mortgage matters.
  • Property equity matters.
  • Borrower behavior matters.
  • Bankruptcy matters.
  • Lien position matters.
  • Servicing history matters.
  • Documentation matters.
  • And the available resolution strategy matters.

Second mortgages require a different approach than traditional first-lien mortgage investing.

Our experience managing these variables allows us to evaluate second-lien opportunities from both an investment and asset-management perspective.

Our Approach

Three Disciplines

Disciplined Acquisition

02
