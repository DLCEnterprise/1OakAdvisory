Sell Your Loans to 1 Oak

A Straightforward Path From Loan Tape to Closing

Selling residential mortgage assets shouldn't require an unnecessarily complicated process.

1 Oak Advisory works directly with sellers to evaluate opportunities, conduct due diligence, establish pricing, and move qualified transactions toward closing.

Our Acquisition Process

From Tape to Transfer

1

Submit Your Loan Tape

Send us basic loan-level information for the assets you are considering selling.

For an initial review, we typically focus on information such as:

  • Unpaid Principal Balance
  • Interest Rate
  • Payment
  • Origination Date
  • Property Address
  • Estimated Property Value
  • First Mortgage Balance
  • Lien Position
  • Payment Status
  • Last Payment Date
  • Credit Information, when available
  • Servicing Status

Additional information may be requested depending upon the asset. A summary-level tape is enough at this stage; borrower names, Social Security numbers and account numbers are exchanged later, under a non-disclosure agreement, as part of due diligence.

2

Initial Review

Our team evaluates the opportunity based on the characteristics of the loan, collateral, lien position, payment performance, documentation, and expected resolution strategy.

Because we actively manage residential mortgage assets, our analysis extends beyond a traditional performing-loan model.

3

Indicative Pricing

Following our initial review, we can provide preliminary or indicative pricing for assets that meet our acquisition criteria.

This allows sellers to determine quickly whether moving forward makes economic sense.

4

Due Diligence

Once preliminary terms are accepted, we move into loan-level due diligence. Our objective is to identify material issues early and communicate clearly throughout the process.

A non-disclosure agreement is signed before diligence begins. Loan files — including borrower-level detail such as names, Social Security numbers, account numbers, and credit and servicing records — are exchanged through a secure method agreed between the parties.

Depending upon the transaction, our review may include:

  • Note and Mortgage/Deed of Trust
  • Assignment Chain
  • Allonges and Endorsements
  • Payment History
  • Servicing Records
  • Title
  • Property Valuation
  • Credit Information
  • Bankruptcy History
  • Foreclosure Status
  • Modification Agreements
  • Relevant Correspondence
  • Other Material Loan Documentation
5

Closing & Transfer

Once due diligence and final pricing are completed, the parties execute the appropriate transaction documents and coordinate funding, collateral delivery, assignments, and servicing transfer.

  • Transaction Documents
  • Funding
  • Collateral Delivery
  • Assignments
  • Servicing Transfer
