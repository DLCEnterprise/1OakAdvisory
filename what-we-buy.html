What We Buy

Residential Second Mortgages & HELOCs

We evaluate performing and non-performing assets across a range of credit profiles, seasoning, and resolution scenarios.

1 Oak Advisory specializes in acquiring residential mortgage assets with a particular focus on second mortgages and home equity lines of credit.

Acquisition Focus

Our Primary Acquisition Focus

We evaluate performing and non-performing assets across a range of credit profiles, seasoning, payment histories, and resolution scenarios.

Closed-End Second Mortgages

We actively evaluate closed-end residential second mortgages, including recently originated and seasoned assets.

Home Equity Lines of Credit — HELOCs

We acquire HELOCs across a range of performance profiles and seasoning.

Performing Loans

We evaluate performing residential second liens individually and in pools, including seasoned assets with established payment histories.

Non-Performing Loans

Non-performing residential second mortgages are a core area of our experience. Our background in loss mitigation, modifications, bankruptcy, foreclosure, and asset resolution allows us to evaluate non-performing assets based on more than traditional performing-loan metrics.

Early Payment Defaults — EPDs

Early payment defaults can create unique challenges for originators. 1 Oak provides originators with another potential exit.

Scratch-and-Dent Assets

Documentation, servicing history, payment issues, credit changes, or other exceptions can make otherwise valuable mortgage assets difficult to sell through traditional channels. We evaluate scratch-and-dent opportunities individually based on the underlying collateral, borrower profile, documentation, lien position, servicing history, and available resolution strategies.

Seasoned Residential Mortgage Assets

We also evaluate seasoned residential mortgage assets that may no longer fit an institution's portfolio strategy.

For Originators

Another Potential Exit for Originators

A recently originated loan that becomes delinquent may no longer fit traditional secondary-market execution, warehouse, or investor parameters.

We evaluate recently originated residential second mortgages and HELOCs that experience early payment problems, including loans requiring specialized servicing and loss-mitigation strategies.
