Originators & Institutional Sellers

Build a Direct Secondary-Market Relationship With 1 Oak

A dependable direct counterparty for institutions that need liquidity, execution, and flexibility.

1 Oak Advisory is actively expanding direct acquisition relationships with mortgage originators, banks, credit unions, funds, and other institutions that originate or hold residential second mortgages and HELOCs.

For Mortgage Originators

Establish the Exit Before You Need It

Originators don't need every loan to become a problem before establishing an alternative exit strategy. Second mortgages and HELOCs can experience:

  • Early Payment Defaults
  • Credit Deterioration
  • Servicing Issues
  • Documentation Exceptions
  • Investor Fallout
  • Warehouse Issues
  • Repurchase Situations
  • Scratch-and-Dent Classification
  • Other Secondary-Market Exceptions

When those situations occur, having an established buyer relationship can create another potential path to liquidity. 1 Oak wants to establish that relationship before you need it.

How Selling to 1 Oak Works

Forward Flow

Forward-Flow & Recurring Acquisition Relationships

For institutions producing residential second mortgages or HELOCs on an ongoing basis, we are interested in discussing recurring acquisition arrangements. The result can be a more efficient transaction process over time.

A direct relationship allows both parties to become familiar with:

  • Product Guidelines
  • Underwriting Characteristics
  • Documentation
  • Servicing
  • Pricing Expectations
  • Due-Diligence Requirements
  • Closing Procedures

Banks & Credit Unions

Assets That No Longer Align With Portfolio Objectives

Financial institutions may hold residential mortgage assets that no longer align with their portfolio objectives. 1 Oak provides an experienced counterparty capable of evaluating these assets individually or as a portfolio.

This can include:

  • Non-Performing Loans
  • Classified Assets
  • Seasoned Second Mortgages
  • HELOC Portfolios
  • Small-Balance Loans
  • Charged-Off or Previously Charged-Off Assets
  • Loans Requiring Specialized Servicing
  • Assets Requiring Active Loss Mitigation
